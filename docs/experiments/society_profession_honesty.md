# society_profession_honesty

family: society

## Why ask this
Every year Gallup asks Americans to rate the honesty and ethical standards of people in different professions, and every year nurses come out near the top and car salespeople near the bottom. It's the standard picture of which jobs the public trusts.

A model talks about professions constantly: in career advice, in stories, in answers about who to trust. It carries its own ladder of trust, which may be kinder or harsher than the public's, and more or less extreme.

## The people and the data
**Gallup's Honesty and Ethics in Professions** poll asks US adults, by phone and online, to rate people in each field from "very low" to "very high". We use the December 2025 poll for 16 professions; 15 are shown here. The people's answer is Gallup's published split for each.

## What Jev was asked
Gallup's own question and answers:

> How would you rate the honesty and ethical standards of people in this field: building contractors?
> *Very low honesty and ethical standards · Low honesty and ethical standards · Average honesty and ethical
> standards · High honesty and ethical standards · Very high honesty and ethical standards*

## How we measured it
For each profession, the share rating it high or very high: Gallup's figure against Jev's probability on those two answers. We check whether the order of professions matches (rank correlation: 1 means the same order) and how far apart the two are on average and at the ends.

## Caveats
- **Jev answered as a single person.** A single confident answer will look more extreme than a crowd's average, so part of the "starker ladder" is that difference in kind.
- **Professions left out.** We left out members of Congress, journalists, police officers, clergy and labor union leaders to keep politics and religion off the site, and a content filter hid one more (military veterans). The ladder here is the less contested middle of Gallup's list.
- **The poll's wording, the poll's scale.** Jev got Gallup's exact question and its five answers, from "very low" to "very high". Gallup's "no opinion" answers are dropped from the people's side, since Jev couldn't give one.
