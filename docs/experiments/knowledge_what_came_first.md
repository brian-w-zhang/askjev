# knowledge_what_came_first

family: knowledge

## Why ask this
Knowing roughly when something happened is a sign of how densely a model has read about it. Holding the gap in years fixed and switching what's being dated shows which worlds it knows in fine detail and which only in outline.

## The people and the data


## What Jev was asked
Two-option questions:

> Which internet meme appeared first: "Please, come to Brazil" or "Draw My Life"?
> *Draw My Life: type of internet video · Please, come to Brazil: Brazilian internet meme*

Each was also asked with the two options in the other order.

## How we measured it
Accuracy by the gap in years between the two things, and, for pairs 1 to 5 years apart, by kind of thing, with 90% intervals.

## Caveats
- **Dates are recalled, not given.** TypeSafe documents that Jev is weak at comparing dates written in the question. Here the dates aren't given; Jev has to remember them, so this measures memory of when things happened.
- **Disputed dates.** When an internet meme "appeared" or a historical state was "founded" can be arguable, and Wikidata picks one date. Some misses on memes may be the key's fault.
- **Small group for memes.**
