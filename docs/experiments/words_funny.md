# words_funny

family: words

## Why ask this
Some words are funny on their own. "Nincompoop", "gaggle", "tinkle" make people smile before they mean anything, through their sounds, their associations, or a hint of rudeness, and psychologists have collected ratings of how funny thousands of words are.

Jev has trouble telling which joke or cartoon caption people find funnier (see "Jev can't tell which New Yorker captions are funny"). Single words strip humor down to sound and meaning. If Jev can tell a funny word, the problem with jokes lies elsewhere, in timing, context or surprise.

## The people and the data
Engelthaler and Hills (2018) asked people to rate 4,997 English words for how funny they are, on a scale from 1 (humorless) to 5 (humorous), about 35 raters per word. The ratings are free for non-profit research.

## What Jev was asked
One question per word, with five described answers written for this project:

> How funny is the word "landslide" on its own?
> *Not funny at all: an ordinary word with nothing amusing about it · Barely funny: most people wouldn't notice
> anything amusing · Somewhat funny: it might raise a small smile · Funny: many people would find it amusing to say or
> hear · Very funny: a word people giggle at just for how it sounds or what it means*

Each was also asked with the levels reversed (averaged) and for "most people".

## How we measured it
We rank the words by Jev's answer and by people's average and compare the rankings (a rank correlation: 1 means the same order), then list the words where the two rankings differ most.

## Caveats
- **Averages only.** The study published each word's average rating and its spread, not every answer, so we compare rankings, not how often each rating was given.
- **Different scales and wording.** People rated from 1 (humorless) to 5 (humorous). The comparison uses rankings for that reason.
- **A slice of the list.** We asked 599 of the study's 4,997 words, spread evenly from least to most funny; 8 were hidden by the content filter that keeps sensitive words off the site. Funny words are overrepresented compared with the full list.
- **Middle-of-the-road words.** Jev calls them flatly not funny. Part of the "less amused" list may be a different use of the bottom of the scale, not a different sense of humor.
